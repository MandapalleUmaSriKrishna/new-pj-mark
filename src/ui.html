How to play

🐭 You are Cheddar the mouse

Grab all the cheese in the level, then dash to your mouse hole before Whiskers the cat catches you!

🕹️ 3D worlds

Move with WASD or ↑←↓→  •  Space hop (hops over traps!)  •  Shift sprint

🏃 2D worlds

←→ run  •  Space jump (press twice for double-jump) — outrun the cat chasing you!

⚠️ Watch out

Mousetraps snap you in place (and the cat hears it!). Hopping over them is safe. Lose all ❤️❤️❤️ and it's game over.

Other keys

P/Esc pause  •  R restart level  •  M sound on/off

📱 Touch

On phones & tablets an on-screen joystick and buttons appear automatically.
